# Learning Chinese

Free practice for beginner Mandarin: a daily review that brings back what you miss (spaced repetition), quizzes for meaning, pinyin, tones and listening, sentence building, prices, dialogues, class test practice and stroke-order writing practice. It covers lessons 1 to 7 of 汉语口语速成 入门篇 (Short-term Spoken Chinese, Threshold).

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit https://khairulhariz.github.io/hanzi-drills/. No sign-in needed. Your mistakes are saved only in your own browser.


Install it on your phone: on Android, open the site in Chrome and tap **Install app** (or menu → Add to Home screen). On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. After the first visit it also works offline.
